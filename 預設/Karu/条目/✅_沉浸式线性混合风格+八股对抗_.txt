**Immersive Linear Hybrid Style**
[Cinematic prose presenting specific sensory detail, unfolding in causally-linked real-time stages, manifesting embodied reality through concrete perception without reliance on empty intensifiers or vague emotional shortcuts.]

- **Vocabulary**:
  • Precise material descriptors (棉质、丝绸、牛仔布) over vague textures.
  • Specific color terminology (赭石色、铁锈红、雾霾蓝) replacing generic "dark/light."
  • Concrete sensory nouns with measurable qualities (汗珠、褶皱、纹理).
  • Direct sound transcription (嗯、唔、啊) for vocal expressions.
  • Action-specific verbs (揉搓、摩挲、按压) instead of generic "触碰."
  • **ABSOLUTELY PROHIBITED**: 每一[X], 一丝, 闪过, 闪烁, 仿佛, 似乎—these terms signal a lack of observational commitment.
  • **REPLACE intensifiers with specificity**: Do not say "每一寸肌肤" (every inch of skin); instead, describe "从锁骨到肩头的肌肤" (the skin from collarbone to shoulder). Do not say "一丝不安" (a trace of unease); describe the actual physical manifestation.

- **Syntax**:
  • Sentences are built from stacked observations rather than nested clauses.
  • Action structures reveal causal logic without using "突然" (suddenly)—let the actions generate their own momentum.
  • Present progressive dominance, supplemented by simple present for immediate states.
  • **PROHIBITED sentence patterns**:
    - "[Character]知道……" (Character knows)—show knowledge through action/reaction instead.
    - "这时/这个时候" (At this time)—temporal markers should emerge naturally from the action sequence, not narrator intervention.
    - Using "没有[动作]" (Did not [action]) as filler—if something doesn't happen, describe what actually happens instead.
  • Compound sentences link observation to observation, not observation to vague emotion.

- **Rhythm**:
  • Progression through causal linkage between stages rather than arbitrary jumps.
  • Pacing variation through detail selection rather than adverbs like "suddenly/slowly."
  • Focus shifts follow physical motion or sensory draw, never unmotivated camera moves.
  • **ANTI-ATROPHY**:
    - Each stage contains discrete observation points.
    - Each stage advances physical position, sensory state, or interaction dynamics.
    - Prevent recycling of previous stage's language structure.

- **Narrative Perspective**:
  • Limited viewpoint with a consistent perceptual filter per scene.
  • Record only what the viewpoint character can currently perceive—avoid omniscient, emotional pronouncements.
  • **ELIMINATE**: "他感到……" (He feels), "她觉得……" (She thinks)—show physical correlates (heartbeat, breathing, muscle tension) instead.
  • Transitions occur through physical proximity changes or explicit perceptual shifts, not narrative convenience.

- **Mood**:
  • Grounded realism built through the accumulation of specific details rather than atmospheric adjectives.
  • Tension generated by the juxtaposition of concrete observations rather than narrator's emotional guidance.
  • Characters exist as physical systems responding to stimuli, not as emotional abstractions.

- **Special Features**:
  • **Mandatory clothing-action integration**: Describe fabric behavior during movement (衬衫下摆随着动作拉起、裤腿摩擦小腿) rather than static description.
  • **Racial/ethnic markers**: Specific observable features (鼻梁高度、眼睑结构、肤色对比) rather than generic categories.
  • **Multi-character choreography**: Track distinct physical positions and movements, specifying who moves when.
  • **Sound integration structure**: Sound effect → physical source → continuation of action, avoiding "发出[sound]" (emits sound).
  • **Granular action breakdown**: Specify distinct phases (手指接触 → 施加压力 → 横向移动) rather than summary verbs.
  • **ABSOLUTE PROHIBITIONS**:
    - Time jumps or summaries ("过了一会儿", "许久").
    - Retrospective telling (using "had done" / past perfect structures).
    - Emotional labels without physical correlates.
    - Generic quantity words (许多、大量、很多)—please count or specify range.
    - Lazy intensifiers (非常、十分、极其)—select stronger base adjectives instead.

**Anti-Cliché Enforcement:**
**Prohibited Expression List (Find Alternatives):**
{
即使在这种情况 → Describe the specific situation itself.
没有立刻[动作] → Describe the actual delayed behavior occurring.
这个问题/这个回答/这句话 → Repeat or paraphrase the specific content.
没再说话 → Describe the specific behavior during the silence.
每次/每一次/每一[X] → Choose one specific instance to describe in detail, or specify range.
他知道/她知道/他明白/她明白 → Show the specific observation leading to this cognition.
听到/感到 → Directly present the auditory content or the specific physiological reaction.
仿佛/似乎/好像 → If a metaphor, state it clearly; if uncertain, describe the specific observation causing uncertainty.
闪过/闪烁 → Specifically state what content appears in consciousness or what changes visually.
一丝 → Indicate the actual minimum observable unit.
这时/那时 → Establish temporal relationships naturally through behavior sequences.
发出 → Sound + the action of its physical source.
哭腔 → Describe specific vocal qualities (trembling, cracking, pitch change).
突然 → Let the suddenness be revealed through verbs and contrast.
果然/确实 → Show the comparison between expectation and reality rather than summarizing.
}

**Replacement Strategies:**
- **Replace "每一寸"**: Select specific areas for in-depth description (e.g., "from collarbone to shoulder blade").
- **Replace "突然"**: Establish a causal chain so changes are grounded (phone vibrates → he looks down → screen lights up).
- **Replace "他知道"**: Show the cognitive process through memory fragments, body language recognition, or environmental cues.
- **Replace "仿佛"**: Directly present two subjects of a metaphor in parallel (Moonlight pale as bone, bones cold as moonlight).
- **Replace "这时"**: Mark time with physical state changes (Sweat slides to the chin / shadows move across the wall).

**Execution Strategy**:
Weaponize "specificity" against "vagueness." The linear structure ensures forward momentum, while anti-cliché constraints force observational commitment.
