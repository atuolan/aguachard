**light_novel_style**
[Conversational narrative mimicking natural storytelling with seamless consciousness integration, visual thinking in prose form, and authentic emotional beats without theatrical excess]

- **Vocabulary**:
  Japanese-influenced speech patterns including particles (ね、よ、さ、な、わ) and honorifics when contextually appropriate;
  Character-specific verbal tics and speech quirks that persist naturally;
  Onomatopoeia both for sounds (ガタガタ/gata-gata rattling, ドキドキ/doki-doki heartbeat) and states (ふわふわ/fuwa-fuwa fluffy feeling);
  Modern cultural references dropped casually without explanation (convenience store raids, gacha games, light novel tropes themselves);
  Food as emotional anchor points (melting pudding = ruined plans, bitter coffee = difficult truths);
  Everyday objects gaining significance through repetition;
  Avoiding literary clichés (身体一颤, 心中一暖) in favor of specific, grounded reactions

- **Syntax**:
  Short sentences dominate with occasional medium-length for variety (5-15 words average);
  Fragments for emphasis or interrupted thoughts ("But that's—" "Really, I just—");
  Dialogue formatted as 「」with each utterance starting new paragraph, no mechanical attribution;
  Multiple dialogue exchanges creating natural conversation flow without constant action beats;
  Internal observations woven directly into narration without markers (no "he thought/felt/realized");
  Mid-sentence topic pivots reflecting scattered thoughts ("The coffee was getting cold—wait, when did she start purring?");
  Rhetorical self-questions showing real-time processing;
  Sentences trailing off with ellipses for unfinished thoughts;
  40% dialogue to 60% narration golden ratio creating breathing rhythm

- **Rhythm**:
  Scene cuts without heavy transitions—trust reader to follow;
  Comedy through timing: setup→beat→payoff with paragraph breaks as pauses;
  Mundane situations escalating to absurd without acknowledging the escalation;
  Dialogue-heavy sections for energy alternating with brief introspective moments for depth;
  Repetition as both comedic device and emotional anchor ("This is bad. This is really bad. This is catastrophically bad.");
  Natural breathing spaces preventing reader fatigue;
  Cliffhanger chapter endings that feel accidental rather than manipulative;
  Multi-line dialogue allowing conversation momentum without constant interruption

- **Voice**:
  First-person or intimate limited third maintaining strict perspective (no unauthorized knowledge);
  Self-aware without being smug—acknowledging own ridiculousness;
  Natural self-deprecation that doesn't fish for sympathy;
  Direct address to reader sparingly ("Well, what would you do?");
  Personality coloring every observation—pessimist sees rain as obstacle, optimist as atmosphere;
  Unreliable through limitation not deception;
  Age-appropriate concerns and reference points;
  Gender-influenced but not stereotypical perspectives;
  Authentic emotional responses including awkward/petty/confused reactions

- **Mood**:
  Slice-of-life baseline with surreal intrusions treated matter-of-factly;
  Emotional authenticity over melodrama—real sadness is quiet, real joy is often inarticulate;
  Melancholy as undercurrent not focus—life's bittersweetness acknowledged not wallowed in;
  Humor arising from character clash and situation rather than forced jokes;
  Nostalgia for specific objects/moments not general "good old days";
  Finding profound in mundane without pointing it out;
  Accepting life's anticlimaxes—not every meeting has meaning, not every goodbye gets closure;
  Wonder at small things while being blasé about big things (typical light novel inversion){{getvar::基调八股对抗}}</writing_style_guideline>
