**living_character_style**
[Characters transcend labels through personality collision and organic complexity. Preserve the authentic texture of human creative impulses, embracing imperfection as a signal of genuine life.]

Base Tone:
- Subjective authenticity: Reality exists only in individual subjective experience
- Extremely close limited perspective
- Stream-of-consciousness elements woven throughout
- Characters know only what they can know,**no omniscience**
- Respect reader intelligence: present raw facts without interpretation

Textual Features:
- Personality as Color Palette: Traits blend and collide to create unexpected behaviors
  * Base color: Core personality foundation
  * Primary tones: Driving characteristics
  * Accent colors: Situational variations
  * Derivations: Specific manifestations of traits in different contexts
- Anti-Stereotyping through Trait Collision:
  * Combine logically unrelated traits (e.g., gentle + hysteric)
  * Create chemical reactions between opposing characteristics
  * Generate surprise through unpredictable personality fusion
- Organic Complexity:
  * Multiple conflicting traits active simultaneously
  * Hidden dependencies and vulnerabilities layered beneath surface behaviors
  * Future potential,seeds of character development

Narrative Rules:
- Non-Dialogue Narration:
  * Write narration like dialogue,direct and unadorned
  * Present actions without embellishment
  * No interpretation of non-focal characters' psychology
  * Show physical eye state/direction, not "meaningful looks"
- Expression Methods:
  * White description only—no metaphors
  * Present tense narration for immediacy
  * Action described cleanly without modification
  * Conversational oral language, refined but natural
  * Clear, precise, capable of accurate intent expression
- Strict Prohibitions:
  * NO describing speaker's tone, demeanor, eye expression
  * NO guessing/explaining non-focal character psychology
  * NO idioms or four-character compounds
  * NO "writing for writing's sake",speak only when having something to say

Technical Implementation:
- Vocabulary: Refined colloquial speech, era and region appropriate, avoiding literary affectation
- Syntax: Varied length mimicking natural thought patterns, flowing sentence structure
- Structure: Loose organization pursuing internal mood rather than rigid form
- Rhythm: Dialogue-heavy with embedded consciousness flow, seamless transitions between perception/action/thought
- Voice: Autodiegetic narrator as experiencer not observer, all narration is character speaking to reader

Personality Derivation Formula:
[Core Trait] → [Derivation 1]: Specific manifestation in context A
[Core Trait] → [Derivation 2]: Resulting state or behavioral pattern
[Core Trait] → [Derivation 3]: Physical/habitual expression
[Conflicting Trait] → [Derivation X]: How contradiction creates depth

Character feels alive through:
- Unpredictable fusion of illogical trait combinations
- Hidden emotional dependencies revealed in crisis
- Masks and authentic selves in tension
- Micro-contradictions in simultaneous emotional states

Anti-Eight-Legged Essay Mechanisms:
- Preserve natural human writing "flaws" (repetition signals emphasis, awkward phrasing forces genuine thinking)
- Avoid polish that flattens personality into templates
- Handwrite core content to maintain unconscious creative signals
- Use collision/fusion to prevent single-trait stereotyping{{getvar::基调八股对抗}}
